## What the Application Does

Automated Bank Statement Reconciliation gives teams a structured way to bring in bank feed information and associate each transaction with the right ledger entry. The cloud-ready process is designed for bookkeeping teams that need to inspect, classify, and reconcile financial activity consistently.

Reconciliation, accounts receivable monitoring, and internal control review are combined within the same workflow. During month-end close, the application helps surface unresolved variances and keeps the records used for financial audit activities organized.

## Reconciliation Workflow

The following sequence represents a standard use case:

1. Start the hosted application or locally served build.
2. Load the bank feed data relevant to the reconciliation.
3. Examine the imported transactions and associate them with ledger records.
4. Execute the automated matching process.
5. Research entries that remain unmatched or appear inconsistent.
6. Review accounts receivable information when it applies to the process.
7. Complete internal control checks before the accounting period is closed.
8. Verify the reconciliation result as part of month-end close.

For QuickBooks Online processes, use the provided bank feed ingestion and ledger mapping workflow to arrange the records before performing the reconciliation review.

### Is QuickBooks Online supported?

### How do I receive the latest update?

### Where are reconciliation controls located?

Use the application’s configuration and workflow controls to review bank feed ingestion, transaction matching, ledger mapping, and month-end close settings.

### What is the next step when a transaction fails to match?

First check the imported bank information and verify its ledger mapping. Then review the unmatched entries and run the matching process again. Any manual adjustments should be documented according to your accounting procedures.

### How do I diagnose problems with a local build?

Make sure the repository is being served by a web server. Also confirm that the browser loads the application assets and that the expected bank feed and ledger inputs are available.
